## What is Reverse Silo Linking?

**Traditional Silo:** Homepage → Category page → Individual pages (one-way flow)

**Reverse Silo:** Every page links to every other related page (multi-directional mesh)

### Why It's Powerful for SEO:
1. **Distributes Page Authority:** Link juice flows between all industry pages
2. **Captures Adjacent Searches:** User searching for "HVAC software" sees links to electrical, plumbing, etc.
3. **Increases Time on Site:** More internal links = more pages visited
4. **Reduces Bounce Rate:** Users explore related content instead of leaving
5. **Creates Topical Authority:** Google sees comprehensive coverage of field service trades
6. **Improves Crawlability:** Search bots discover all pages more efficiently
